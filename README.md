# AI job search

An AI-assisted job search you run from Claude Code. Your résumé becomes a structured
profile; a daily scan finds and scores new postings against it; and Claude helps you tailor
materials and track every application. Structured JSON files are the single source of truth,
so everything is inspectable and yours.

## What it does

- **Find jobs:** searches, scores each posting against your profile, dedupes, and emails a digest.
- **Tailor applications:** an editable `.docx` résumé, a cover letter, and fit-notes per job.
- **Track your pipeline:** a kanban board of applications, with status detected from your email.
- **Hosted or local tracker:** a web app on your laptop, or optionally on your phone.
- **LinkedIn analysis:** warm introductions, recruiter inbound, and your application funnel.

## Start here

Read **[SETUP.md](SETUP.md)**. About 30 minutes gets you the core; each optional tier adds
about 20.

## Costs

A Claude Pro or Max subscription for Claude Code. The optional daily scan uses your own
Anthropic API key at roughly $0.50 to $1.70 per run, with a monthly limit you set. The
pipeline scan costs $0.

## Privacy

Your copy of this repo **must be private**: your résumé data, jobs, and applications are
committed to it. Nothing is ever auto-submitted, and the only email this sends is your own
digest, to you.
